Mixpert Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Mixpert is an ear-training app for learning to hear and correct EQ. It is published by Jacob Yoo Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • There's no account, and your training progress stays on your device.
  • Songs you import or pick from your library are played on your device and never uploaded.
  • Apple processes all payments. We never see your card details.
  • Our subscription provider, Superwall, receives limited device and usage data so it can show subscription options and confirm what you've unlocked.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Data that stays on your device

Mixpert stores the following locally on your iPhone or iPad. It is not sent to us:

  • Lesson progress, stars, scores, high scores, streaks and badges.
  • Practice sessions you create, your settings and app preferences.
  • Songs and playlists you add to the Library.

If iCloud device backups are on, this data may be included in your device backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Music and media library access

If you choose to add songs from your media library, Mixpert asks for permission to access it. We use this access only to let you pick songs and play them through the app's EQ. Songs and audio files you import are processed on your device and never uploaded. You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

Mixpert Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages billing, renewals, refunds and cancellations. We do not receive your name, email or payment details from Apple.

We use Superwall to show subscription screens and to check whether your subscription is active. To do this, Superwall's software in the app sends Superwall:

  • A random identifier created by the app (not your name, email or Apple ID).
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, region and time zone.
  • Events about subscription screens and purchases, such as which locked feature opened a subscription screen (for example, a lesson unit or song import), when the screen is shown or closed, which plan was selected, and whether a trial or purchase started, completed or was restored.
  • Your IP address, which is part of any internet connection.

We use this information to show the right subscription options, unlock Pro features, and understand which subscription screens work. Superwall handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Mixpert schedules practice and streak reminders on your device. If you start a free trial, a reminder is scheduled on your device to tell you before the trial ends. These notifications are created locally and don't require sending your data to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's Settings.

Feedback and reviews

The app includes optional links to leave an App Store review or to post feedback on our feedback board, which is hosted by Canny. These open outside the app, and anything you submit there is handled by Apple or Canny under their own privacy policies (Apple, Canny). We can see feedback you post on the board.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.
  • We don't require an account or ask for your name or email in the app.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Superwall keeps the data described above for as long as needed to provide its service and as described in its privacy policy. To ask us to request deletion of data associated with your app identifier, email us at the address below. Because we don't collect your name or email in the app, we may ask for details that help us find the right records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict how it's used. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children

Mixpert is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll change the effective date at the top, and for significant changes we'll give notice in the app or on the App Store listing.
